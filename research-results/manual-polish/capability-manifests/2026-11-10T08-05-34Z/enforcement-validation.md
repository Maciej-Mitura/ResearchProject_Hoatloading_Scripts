## Regression-discovered output cleanup repair
The required large-output regression initially failed and quarantined the test process's capacity. Failure logs are retained (initial, isolated, output-bound, CLI-reaping and workload-stop stages). Continuing to drain a million-line flood until wall timeout kept Docker's attach/removal path busy. The current supervisor stops a flooding job as soon as bounded capture overflows, reports failureCategory=output_limit and unavailable final audit framing, stops the container before disconnecting attach, reaps the Docker CLI and drains process handles, and uses a separately bounded 10-second removal interval. Docker's redundant unbounded daemon log is disabled; bounded application capture remains. These are current post-research operational changes, not alterations to frozen experiment artifacts or deterministic v1 rules. The execution manifest records outputLimitAction=stop and dockerLogDriver=none. The complete Docker regression suite then passed 22/22.
