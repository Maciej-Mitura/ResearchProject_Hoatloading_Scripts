# Design decisions

- Use 4 px corners on small controls, 8 px on primary panels, and 12 px on dialogs. Keep boundaries around controls, the editor, tables and important warnings.
- Group settings, permissions, filters and host activity with dividers and spacing. Keep native select menus and their keyboard behavior.
- Keep Run prominent. Style file deletion as destructive and review and export as secondary.
- Use neutral surfaces for chat. Retain the visible difference between user and assistant messages.
- Keep completed, failed, stopped, and collection states distinct. Use a neutral marker when an advisory record returns. The assessment label and its details remain visible.
- Keep the top-level execution diagnosis visible and collapse technical diagnostics by default.
